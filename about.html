What is NewBatteries?

NewBatteries is developing an evidence-led battery identification and supplier-connection platform for the Australian market.

What works in the current prototype

  • Guided battery-information collection in the Battery Finder.
  • Local battery-code recognition from reference data.
  • Preliminary identification with visible unknowns and limitations.
  • Local identification summary generation that you can copy, print or save.
  • Compatibility prototype and Physical Fit Lab demonstration.

What is not active yet

  • Photo upload.
  • Online request submission.
  • Manual technical review.
  • Supplier routing.
  • Live stock, pricing, installation booking or recycling referral workflows.

Current stage

This is an early-stage prototype. The current prototype helps users structure battery information, recognise supported battery codes locally and prepare a summary that can be copied, printed or saved. Online submission, photo upload, manual review and supplier routing are not yet active.
