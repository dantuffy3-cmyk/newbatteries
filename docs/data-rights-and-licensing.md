# Data Rights and Licensing

## Rights model

Rights are tracked independently from evidence authority.

Supported values:

- `publicly_viewable`
- `downloadable`
- `openly_licensed`
- `commercial_reuse_permitted`
- `commercial_reuse_restricted`
- `agreement_required`
- `automated_access_restricted`
- `unknown`

## Important distinctions

- Public visibility does **not** imply reuse rights.
- Downloadability does **not** imply open licence.
- Open licence status must be explicitly verified.
- Commercial reuse cannot be assumed without explicit evidence.
- Some sources may require agreements even if visible online.

## Legal/licensing review triggers

Formal legal review is required when:

- Standards content may be licensed/copyrighted.
- Manufacturer catalogues or manuals have unclear reuse terms.
- Commercial reuse status is unknown but integration is proposed.
- Automated access restrictions are unclear.

## Recommended actions

- `integrate`
- `reference`
- `manually_verify`
- `seek_licence`
- `monitor`
- `avoid`
- `investigate_further`
