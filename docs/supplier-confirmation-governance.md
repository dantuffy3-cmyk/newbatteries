# Supplier confirmation governance

Suppliers can strengthen a physical-fit assessment, but they cannot casually override a confirmed blocking safety rule.

## Suppliers may

- add new evidence records
- correct incorrect input values when stronger evidence is available
- resolve unknowns with clearer measurements or photos
- confirm conditional results with reliable fitment evidence
- reject candidates that still fail their review
- escalate conflicts for technical review

## Suppliers may not

- turn a confirmed blocking conflict into a positive result without changed evidence
- remove the audit trail for earlier evidence
- claim electrical or chemistry compatibility from this physical-fit layer alone
- treat family-code recognition as final proof of fit

## When a blocked result may change

A blocked result may change only when at least one of the following is true:

1. the source input changes
2. stronger evidence corrects an incorrect value
3. the rule is shown not to apply
4. the category profile was incorrect
5. an authorised rule revision is approved and audited

## Audit expectations

Any change from `blocked` to another outcome should retain:

- the original rule ID
- the earlier evidence references
- the replacement evidence references
- the reason for the change
- who approved the change and when

This keeps supplier participation useful without weakening safety-first governance.
