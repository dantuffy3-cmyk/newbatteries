# First 50 Records Design Brief

## Purpose

This plan defines the first 50 battery-family records as a governed onboarding set, not a public launch dataset.

## Allocation rationale

- Coin/Button/Hearing aid: 15
- Automotive/Motorcycle: 15
- Power tool platforms: 10
- Household: 5
- Caravan/Marine: 5

This balances high-frequency consumer identification with higher-risk replacement pathways.

## Evidence posture

Planned records are planning-only and remain non-approved until evidence thresholds are met. Unknown technical fields remain `null`.

## Deferred categories

Residential storage remains specialist-only in this sprint due to higher installation/compliance complexity. Detailed compatibility logic is deferred.

## Specialist-only rationale (residential storage)

Residential storage records can involve compliance, installer requirements, and product-program conditions that require specialist review and legal/licensing checks before operational use.
