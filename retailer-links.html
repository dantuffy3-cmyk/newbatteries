Transparency

Retailer links and independence

What exists today, and how future paid referrals would be explained.

Current status

NewBatteries is an early-stage identification prototype. Affiliate referrals are not active. This page does not claim an approved affiliate relationship with any retailer or network.

The separate seller-journey demonstration uses fictional enquiries and researched examples. It does not use your Finder results, confirm compatibility or offer affiliate deals. Links in our battery guides are ordinary information references.

Planned referral approach

Subject to approval, NewBatteries intends to publish relevant battery information and clearly labelled links to participating retailers. A retailer would handle its own sale, stock, fulfilment and purchase terms. We do not currently offer checkout, live prices, live availability or installation bookings.

Before any paid referral goes live, we will identify it near the link, explain that NewBatteries may earn commission, and update the relevant notices to reflect the actual tracking arrangement. No affiliate tracking is activated by this page.

Identification stays separate from sales

A commission must not turn an unknown into a match, override conflicting evidence or remove a verification requirement. Paid placement would be labelled. A retailer listing is not proof that a product suits your equipment.

We will not describe the site as covering every retailer or finding the cheapest battery unless the evidence supports that claim. Commission rates, eligible products and Click & Collect terms are not confirmed here.
